Dayfeed Support

Every day is a celebration. Here's how to get help with the app.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or a special day we're missing — email us and we'll reply within a couple of days.

nevranizamoglu@gmail.com

What to include

  • Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18)
  • The app version, from Settings → About
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Manage or cancel Pro: through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings — we can't cancel it for you.
  • Not getting reminders: check notifications are allowed for Dayfeed in your device settings, and that you've picked interests in the app.
  • Suggest a special day: use "Suggest a Day" inside the app, or email us.

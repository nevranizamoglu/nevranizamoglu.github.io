2. What Kimo does

Kimo performs three things:

  • Caller ID lookup. You enter a Turkish phone number; Kimo returns any name, category, or community spam score attached to it.
  • Spam reporting. You can flag a number as spam, optionally with a short reason. Reports contribute to a public community score against that number.
  • In-app overlay. With your permission, Kimo reads your address book to mark calls and lookups as "in your contacts" or "unknown".

The Service is free up to a daily limit on the free tier; a paid subscription (managed by Apple or Google) lifts that limit.
